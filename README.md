Overview

Marketing platforms are often judged on volume alone. This project looks at volume and cost together, so the platform that drives the most conversions can be compared with the platform that acquires customers most efficiently.

Dataset
Source: Public Kaggle dataset of marketing campaign data 
Size: about 10,000 rows
Scope: 6 marketing platforms
Headline Numbers
Metric	Value
Total ad spend	$43.5M
Total conversions	656K
Platforms compared	6
Metrics
Metric	Definition
Ad Spend	Total amount spent on advertising
Conversions	Number of customers acquired
CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)	Ad Spend ÷ Conversions
ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)	Revenue ÷ Ad Spend

Findings
Google Ads generated the most conversions, but at a relatively high CAC.
TikTok had a lower CAC alongside strong ROAS in this dataset.
LinkedIn had the highest cost per customer among the platforms compared.
Limitations

This is a public dataset, so these results are patterns in the data, not conclusions about the platforms themselves. Real performance depends on industry, audience, budget, and campaign setup.
